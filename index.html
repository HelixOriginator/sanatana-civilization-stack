Eternal Principles

The Eight Pillars of Dharmic Equality

Core philosophical foundations that establish universal equality within Sanatana Dharma — validated by scripture, history, and lived tradition.

☸

Advaita Vedanta

All existence is one divine consciousness. If all beings share the same essential nature, discrimination based on birth becomes philosophically impossible.

⚖

Tat Tvam Asi

"Thou Art That" — The same divine essence pervades the universe and exists within every being. No being is inherently superior or inferior.

🔥

Karma Yoga

Every soul accumulates karma through actions, not birth. Spiritual progress depends on righteousness of deeds — present actions shape destiny.

❤

Bhakti Yoga

Divine love is available to ALL beings. Women, Shudras, and all marginalized groups are explicitly included. Devotion, not birth, determines success.

👁

Sama Darshana

The enlightened see with equal vision a learned Brahmin, a cow, an elephant, a dog, and a dog-eater. True wisdom reveals equality of all beings.

🌍

Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam

"The World is One Family" — Transcends all artificial divisions. For the magnanimous, the entire world is one family. Small-minded people create divisions.
